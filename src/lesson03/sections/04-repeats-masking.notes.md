## Toys (what the JS computes)
- .out explainer: invented contig and family names (toyL1_A, toySINE_B, (TG)n); columns and their meanings follow repeatmasker.help 3.1.
- Landscape builder: three invented families; sequences simulated per site (Poisson events, 2/3 transitions; CpG C/G transitions multiplied by a slider); divergence computed with the exact RepeatMasker loop (port of SearchResult::calcKimuraDivergence). "True" = simulated expected events per non-CpG site.
- TRF toy: substitution-only. For each period d: (1) best stretch where s[i]==s[i+d] more often than not (+1/-1 Kadane); (2) majority consensus per phase; (3) each base scored +2 / -penalty against the majority of the OTHER copies at its phase (leave-one-out, so a 2-copy multiple period cannot match itself), best local stretch kept; (4) reported = shortest period within 10 % of the best score. These are toy rules, not TRF's. Real TRF handles indels (wraparound DP), uses k-tuple statistical criteria (PM/PI) and reports up to three period sizes. Said on the page.
- Landscape toy tuning: CpG fraction of the SINE-like consensus ~7 % CG pairs; default CpG transition multiplier 10x (slider 1-20; RM source comment says 15x in human). Kimura without CpG rule overestimates the CpG-rich family about 2x at 10x; the CpG rule brings it near the simulated age.
