## Interactives (03-discovery.js)
1. Head vs shuffles: fixed random 72-nt toy tRNA + 28-nt body; age slider 0-70 % (substitutions; 1 in 10 changes is a 1-nt indel); Smith-Waterman affine (+5/-4, 12+4k); 20 mononucleotide shuffles (page says real runs use dinucleotide shuffles); control reference = unrelated random 72 nt. Verdict rules copy report_trna.py. Tested (playwright): age 0/20/40 -> tRNA; 50 -> tRNA? (63 % over 59 nt); 60/70 -> none detected; control -> none detected.
2. Step outward: 16 copies, 40-bp body end, candidate end 20 bp before body end, poly(A) 6-14, optional 20-bp extra piece in 6 copies; 10-bp windows, ungapped pair identity, elevated > 45 %; background 300 random windows / 2,000 pairs; share rule = step 7; mean rule = toy stand-in (bg mean + 10 points, labelled as such). 20 seeds per setting: no subgroup, share rule: boundary +30/+40 (true body end +20, tails end +26..+34); subgroup, share rule: mostly +50 (subgroup ends +46..+54) at 5-10 % divergence; subgroup, mean rule: mostly +30. At 30-40 % divergence the share rule with subgroup stops earlier (+30/+40).

## Open doubts / left out

- SINE_Scan SAQ exact definition (BioPerl-based column similarity) not explained beyond "per-column alignment quality score".
- The README vs code mismatches (scan merge distance, scan output names; AnnoSINE_v2 -s/-minc defaults) are reported as found; the bioconda AnnoSINE2 build was not checked and could differ from the GitHub commit.
- Blind test "1 of 6" is from lab notes, not re-measured; shown as unpublished.
- The *R. sinicus* head test is a bat SINE set, outside the talpid families; shown only as a method example with its taxon named.
