# s04 What SNP arrays can and cannot do - notes and sources (2026-11-05)

Read in full text unless marked "abstract only".

- Albrechtsen, Nielsen, Nielsen 2010, Mol Biol Evol 27:2534, doi 10.1093/molbev/msq148. ABSTRACT ONLY (PubMed): "the single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) chip data suffer from ascertainment biases caused by the SNP discovery process in which a small number of individuals from selected populations are used as discovery panels"; "the ascertainment biases will distort measures of human diversity". The paper does not state "rare variants are lost" in the abstract; that is our inference (labelled in the page).
- Palamara et al. 2018, Nat Genet 50:1311, doi 10.1038/s41588-018-0177-x; bioRxiv 276931v1 full text (first ~200k characters read). Quotes: "the observed sites may not be a randomly ascertained subset of polymorphic variants in the sample"; probability "estimated using the ratio between the empirical allele frequency spectrum" of the data and the neutral expectation; monomorphic-site approximation "is not needed when SNP array data are analyzed"; ASMC "can operate either on pairs of unphased chromosomes within a single diploid individual, or on pairs of phased chromosomes across individuals"; cross-individual mode "requires the availability of phased haplotypes, which may not be easy to obtain". Abstract: r2 0.87 on UK Biobank array density (not used on page).
- Zhang, Biddanda, Gunnarsson, Cooper, Palamara 2023, Nat Genet 55:768, doi 10.1038/s41588-023-01379-x, PMC10181934 (first 100k chars read, plus back matter). "The sample IDs output by step 1 inform step 2, in which ASMC is run over pairs of samples."; "We performed reference-free phasing of the remaining variants and samples using Beagle 5.1"; "ARG-derived ultra-rare variants provided stronger tagging compared with imputed ultra-rare variants"; "To obtain realistic SNP data, we subsampled the simulated sequence sites to match the genotype density". 337,464 UK Biobank participants from the PubMed record. No minimum sample size found in the text read; the internal "about 300 haplotypes" claim is NOT used (unverified).
- Nielsen, Vaughn, Deng 2025, Nat Rev Genet 26:47, doi 10.1038/s41576-024-00772-4 (author PDF). "tsinfer-sparse (a variation of tsinfer) and ARG-Needle are the only methods that support genotype array data." Table 1 row for ARG-Needle read in the extracted text only partially (columns unlabelled in extraction); the sentence above is the basis.
- tsinfer manual, https://tskit.dev/tsinfer/docs/stable/inference.html (stable at fetch date): "For each site used for inference we must know the ancestral state."; "excluding by default any sites that are fixed, singletons, or where the ancestral state is unknown". Page says nothing about arrays or a sparse mode. Sparse-mode details NOT verified (open doubt).
- Santiago et al. 2020, Mol Biol Evol 37:3642, doi 10.1093/molbev/msaa169 (OUP page, first 100k chars read). "from chip genotyping or DNA sequencing data"; "with the highest resolution within a hundred generations before sampling"; "The method can be applied from large samples to samples of fewer than ten individuals"; needs a genetic map in cM or a constant recombination rate. Ascertainment bias not discussed in the part read.
- GONE repository https://github.com/esrud/GONE, changelog: "A maximum of 100,000 SNPs are allowed per chromosome."; "A maximum number of 200 chromosomes and a maximum number of 1800 individuals are allowed." Phasing: not stated in the README read (user guide PDF not read); the page therefore does not claim GONE needs no phasing.
- Novo et al. 2023, Genet Sel Evol 55:86, doi 10.1186/s12711-023-00859-2. ABSTRACT ONLY (PubMed): estimates "may be substantially biased when there has been a recent mixture of populations that were previously separated for a long period of time"; also low migration, inversions.
- PSMC README https://github.com/lh3/psmc: "the i-th character in the output sequence indicates whether there is at least one heterozygote in the bin". "Blocked on arrays" is our reasoning (labelled).

## Toy and controls
Folded neutral SFS, n = 100 chromosomes, class k weight 1/k + 1/(n-k) (k = n/2: 1/k). Array = sites with MAF >= cut-off; optional discovery panel of d chromosomes finds a site with probability 1 - (1-p)^d - p^d (p = true frequency; approximation, stated on the page). Tajima's D from the expected spectrum with 1,000 sites.
Controls: (1) in page on load: MAF 0 and no panel returns the full spectrum; Tajima's D of the neutral spectrum = 0; pi = theta for the unfolded 1/i spectrum: all pass. (2) Separate Kingman-coalescent Monte Carlo (n=30, 40,000 replicates): observed expected count of sites in class i vs theta/i: 1.001/1.000, 0.495/0.500, 0.334/0.333, 0.245/0.250, 0.198/0.200; max relative deviation for i<=10 was 2.6 %. Pass. (3) Slider results checked by hand: MAF 5 % keeps 590 of 1000 sites, D = 1.90; panel 20 + MAF 1 % gives D = 1.26.
