## Formula
- Tajima F. 1989. Genetics 123:585-595, doi:10.1093/genetics/123.3.585, PMC1203831. **Only the abstract could be opened** (PMC PDF and OUP page are behind bot checks; tried by agent and in the owner's Chrome). Abstract: "The relationship between the two estimates of genetic variation at the DNA level, namely the number of segregating sites and the average number of nucleotide differences estimated from pairwise comparison, is investigated."
- The formulas (a1, a2, b1, b2, c1, c2, e1, e2, D) were taken from Wikipedia "Tajima's D", section "Mathematical details" (secondary), and checked by two independent computations: Python by the research agent and the JS of this section: D(n=4,S=3,k=1.5) = -0.7545, D(n=10,S=10,k=3) = -0.6707, a1(10) = 2.8290. The page's code control repeats these.
- Watterson's estimator theta_W = S/a_n: Wikipedia "Watterson estimator" (secondary); Watterson 1975 not opened.
- Rule of thumb +-2: Wikipedia: "A very rough rule of thumb to significance is that values greater than +2 or less than -2 are likely to be significant" and "+/- 2 does not actually represent a critical value for a significance test." Page states it as a rough rule only.

## Interpretation
- Nielsen R. 2005. Annu Rev Genet 39:197-218 (PDF opened, two-column extraction): "Simonsen et al. (96) showed that Tajima's D test (112) would reject a neutral model very frequently in the presence of population growth."; "simple models of population subdivision can lead the commonly used neutrality tests to reject the neutral model with high probability"; "if strong departures from the neutral model are seen only on one or a few outlier loci, this may be interpreted as evidence for selection on these loci. However, certain demographic factors, such as population subdivision, may increase the variance".
- Signs (negative = excess of rare variants, positive = deficit of rare / excess of middle-frequency): Wikipedia: "A negative Tajima's D signifies an excess of low frequency polymorphisms relative to expectation"; "A positive Tajima's D signifies low levels of both low and high frequency polymorphisms". Growth -> negative and recent bottleneck -> positive are also shown directly by the coalescent toy (numbers below). Balancing selection -> positive and sweep -> negative are standard interpretations, not quoted from an opened source.
- Ascertainment (array SNPs): Nielsen et al. 2007 (PMC2933187, agent's fetch summary, not verbatim-checked): genotyped data "will be deficient in low-frequency alleles". Section 04 of this lesson shows the effect on a toy.

## Tools (sources opened at the tags given; commands checked by me in the files)
- pixy 2.2.5 (github ksamuk/pixy tag 2.2.5, docs/examples.rst, docs/about.rst, docs/interpreting_tajima_d.rst): example `pixy --stats pi watterson_theta tajima_d --vcf ... --populations ... --window_size 10000 --n_cores 4`; about.rst: "With variants-only VCFs, there is often no way to distinguish missing sites from invariant sites." / "This results in a deflated estimate of π."; interpreting_tajima_d.rst: "*D* is sound for **comparing** windows, populations, or groups computed the same way on the same data", "*D* is **not a p-value**. Its absolute value is not a test of neutrality: under neutrality its expectation is not 0 and its standard deviation is not 1."; versions 2.1.0-2.2.1 shrank |D| with missing data (agent quote).
- vcftools 0.1.17 (src/cpp/variant_file_output.cpp): header `CHROM\tBIN_START\tN_SNPS\tTajimaD`; window pi: `N_monomorphic_sites = window_size - bins[...][N_variant_sites];` (window length used unless --mask); TajimaD n = `N_kept_individuals()*2`; warnings "Only using bialleleic sites", "Only using fully diploid sites" (agent quotes).
- ANGSD 0.942, wiki "Thetas Tajima Neutrality tests.md" (angsd.wiki.git): commands copied (folded variants with -fold 1; "If you do not have the ancestral state you can simply use the assembly you have mapped agains, but remember to add -fold 1"); "only the thetaW and thetaD, and tajimas D is meaningful" (folded; agent quote); output values are sums, nSites = "number of sites with data". On the page the binaries are written without the `./misc/` prefix.
- scikit-allel 1.3.13 (allel/stats/diversity.py): tajima_d `min_sites=3`; n = `ac.sum(axis=1).max()`; sequence_diversity: "If start and/or stop are not provided, uses the difference between the last and the first position as a proxy for the total number of sites, which can overestimate the sequence diversity." (agent quotes).
