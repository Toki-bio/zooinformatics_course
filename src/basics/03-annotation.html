1A repeat is a sequence that occurs again and again

A genome contains the same stretch of DNA many times. In interspersed repeats the copies are scattered; most are transposable elements, pieces of DNA that copy themselves to new places. In tandem repeats the copies lie side by side. The computer marks every run of 10 bases that occurs at least twice.

2Repeat copies can look like genes

A gene finder looks for DNA that resembles genes, and a repeat copy can resemble one. Each toy genome holds one real gene and some copies of one repeat. Masking the repeats first hides them from the finder.

Green: the real gene. Red: a repeat copy reported as a gene. Purple: the repeat copies, which the mask covers.

3A gene is cut into pieces

A gene is made of exons, which stay in the mRNA (the RNA copy used to make the protein), and introns between them, which are cut out. The CDS is the part that codes for the protein.

Thick boxes: CDS. Lines: introns. The third row skips exon 3, another version of the gene.

4RNA reads show where the exons are

The best evidence for a gene is the animal's own RNA, sequenced as short reads and laid on the genome. A read that crosses an exon-exon junction lies in two places: a split read. It shows where an intron was cut out.

Bars: reads per base. Arcs: introns shown by split reads (green: at least 2, orange: 1). Grey: true exons.

5Identical stretches show shared ancestry

An animal carries two copies of each chromosome. If both parents inherited a piece from one ancestor, the two copies are identical along it: a run of homozygosity (ROH). Each strip is one chromosome; bars show differences between the two copies.

Orange: differences. Blue: runs the computer calls (at least 1 Mb). Grey line: the true shared pieces. F ROH is the share of the chromosome inside runs.

6No reads is not no differences

A window with no differences looks homozygous. But where no reads were placed, nobody could see differences. The callable bases are those where reads were good enough to see one. The lower strip uses only those.

Hatched grey: windows with no reads. The animal has one true 8 Mb run.
