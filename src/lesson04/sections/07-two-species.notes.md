## Derived / our wording
- K-bin share 1 - exp(-d b) assumes randomly placed differences (Poisson); 2 % -> 0.86 / 0.33 / 0.18 (checked). 0.2 % het -> 18 % of 100-bp bins.
- "With one unphased genome per species MSMC2 cross runs reduce to the hPSMC pseudo-diploid": our reading of the -I/phasing description, not a statement in the docs.
- BPP prior rule of thumb (theta ~ heterozygosity; tau a bit under half the divergence, since d ≈ 2 tau + theta_anc): our derivation from theta = 4N mu and the MSC, not from the manual. Template priors (invgamma 3 0.004 / 3 0.02) are examples, not our run's priors (those are not in the lab project log) — the page labels the file a template.
- gIMble IM_BA reading ("genes flowed from A into B forward in time") is our translation of "backwards in time ... B->A"; the page says to check the help.
- `-m10` for 20-bp bins and the gIMble bounds are labelled examples.
- Migration-not-evidence warning (misfit can inflate w; simulate without gene flow): our recommendation, consistent with the lab's own cobraa null result (s05) and the notes' verdict "migration call fragile".

## Toy model
Two lineages, one per daughter species (each size Nd); backward migration rate m = Nm/Nd per lineage per generation (symmetric), during the chosen gene-flow window: since split [0, T], first half after split [T/2, T], recent contact [0, 0.1 T]. Same-species pairs coalesce at 1/(2 Nd); older than T both in ancestor, rate 1/(2 NA). Exact 2x2 matrix exponential per grid step (log grid 1e3-3e7 plus exact breakpoints). IICR = 1/(2 hazard). Stepped curve: 24 log windows 12 ky-12 My, window rate = (S(a)-S(b))/∫S. Divergence d = 2 mu E[t]; tail beyond grid added as S(end) x 2NA. Crossing readout: scanning from old to young, the first time the curve exceeds 10x / 1.5x NA. Checked outputs: default (T 1 My, NA 300k, no gene flow) d = 1.73 %, K 82/29/16 %; Nm 0.01-0.1 -> 14-67 % of genome coalesces after the split; Nm 1 -> 1.5x crossing at ~15 ky.
Not modelled: recombination, mutation noise, finite data, the PSMC HMM itself, asymmetric gene flow.
